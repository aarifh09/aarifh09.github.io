07 — How I Work

A rigorous process.

A structured design workflow for telecom electrical and MEPF projects, from the initial brief through to reviewed, issued documentation.

01

Requirements & Brief

Review the project brief, site and equipment requirements, and applicable codes — IEC, NFPA, ASHRAE, NBC, IS and Saudi Civil Defense.

02

Engineering Assessment

Establish the design basis: AC/DC and electrical load estimates, HVAC and lighting assessments, and system and equipment selection.

03

Drawings & Coordination

Prepare layouts, SLDs, cable sizing, earthing and schedules, and coordinate with architectural, civil and structural teams to resolve clashes.

04

Documentation & Review

Assemble design packages with BOQs, transmittals, drawing registers and revision logs; incorporate review comments, redlines and as-built documentation.
